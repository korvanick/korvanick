Power Systems Planning Engineer

Great River Energy · 2022 to 2025 · Maple Grove, Minnesota

Generation and transmission cooperative serving 27 member cooperatives within MISO. I was the primary DER contact, which put me between developers, member cooperatives, internal departments, and the RTO.

Operations Engineer

Otter Tail Power · 2020 to 2022 · Fergus Falls, Minnesota

Operations engineering was me and my manager. Real time support for a control room covering about 5,300 miles of transmission line, most of it 41.6 kV, across western Minnesota and the Dakotas.

  • Ran daily N-1 contingency analysis in Siemens ODMS and validated control room switching orders as the last technical check before execution.
  • Developed and executed a coordinated transformer tap plan across 41.6 kV and 69 kV to mitigate regional voltage problems. Five transformers adjusted, and the plan is still in place as far as I know.
  • Traced widespread low voltage and thermal overloads back to load control restoring demand higher than it otherwise would have been. Worked with the responsible team to change how and when it ran in the affected areas.
  • Diagnosed voltage oscillations caused by a wind plant AVR and pursued the fix with the developer.
  • Took over the sub-100kV network model and facility ratings database, and found a data entry quirk that had been suppressing a few ratings.
  • Led technical troubleshooting on a CIM based ODMS upgrade, clearing data mapping errors that were blocking valid state estimation. Case convergence went from roughly 50 percent to about 90 percent.
  • Supported the underfrequency and undervoltage load shedding programs under NERC PRC-006 and PRC-010, and contributed to the annual review of the regional black start plan.
  • Reconstructed two operational switching errors in the power flow simulator for root cause review. Both became informal training on the monthly operator calls.

Earlier: power electronics research at UMD in 2019 on an EV DC fast charging topology, and two years processing medical claims data at Xerox from 2015 to 2017. I also ran a small LLC from 2021 to 2025 alongside full time work, doing landscaping and running mining hardware.
